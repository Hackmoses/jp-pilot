## Needs a teacher’s check

- **Tannin and tanning hides (`bio-jamb-2015-022`).** The source question concerns a use of plant products. Its nearest group covers substances organisms make, secrete or remove. The clip keeps tannins distinct from animal excretory products.
- **Calvin cycle terminology (`bio-jamb-2017-014`).** The key says “Reduction of carbon dioxide.” Biology 2e §8.3 distinguishes carbon fixation, where Rubisco incorporates carbon dioxide into an organic molecule, from the later reduction of its product. The broader answer points to the Calvin cycle, but the wording should be revised if the intended question is specifically about the fixation step.

The local materials support the procedure, while OpenStax covers the carbohydrate concepts. The clips use qualitative wording, since colour intensity and preparation affect the result.
