Ask about this question Q12 · Why cryolite is added
Why can't they just heat the alumina to 2050 °C?
They could, but keeping a huge cell that hot every day would cost far more fuel and power. Cryolite lets the same electrolysis run at about 960 °C, so aluminium becomes cheap enough to make.
