In the Hall-Héroult process, why is cryolite (Na₃AlF₆) added to the alumina?
WHY B IS CORRECT

Pure alumina melts at about 2050 °C, far too hot to run cheaply. Dissolved in molten cryolite, electrolysis runs at about 960 °C.

THE TRAP

Purifying sounds right, but that happens earlier, in the Bayer process. Cryolite is a solvent, not a cleaner.

REMEMBER IT

Cryolite Cuts the heat. 2050 down to 960.
