What mass of iron is produced when 80 g of Fe₂O₃ is completely reduced by CO in the blast furnace? (Fe = 56, O = 16)

B 56 g
Your answer. Correct. Half a mole of Fe₂O₃ gives one mole of iron, which weighs 56 g.

THE WORKING
  1. Molar mass of Fe₂O₃ = 2(56) + 3(16) = 160 g/mol
  2. Moles of Fe₂O₃ = 80 ÷ 160 = 0.5 mol
  3. Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂, so 0.5 mol gives 1 mol Fe
  4. Mass of iron = 1 × 56 = 56 g
THE TRAP

28 g is the most common wrong answer. It comes from forgetting that one Fe₂O₃ gives two Fe atoms.
