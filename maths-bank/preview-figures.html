Question 25

If x327 = 15, find the value of x.

  1. 3 ✓
    It gives 7 × 3 − 6 = 15.
  2. 5
    It gives 29.
    Substituting 5 gives 7 × 5 − 3 × 2 = 29. Set 7x − 6 equal to 15 before solving for x.
  3. 4
    It gives 22.
    Substituting 4 gives 7 × 4 − 3 × 2 = 22. After adding 6 to both sides, divide 21 by 7.
  4. 2
    It gives 8.
    Substituting 2 gives 7 × 2 − 3 × 2 = 8. The determinant must equal 15, so check the value in 7x − 6.

Answer A. For a 2 × 2 determinant, multiply the main diagonal and subtract the other diagonal’s product. Here that gives 7x − 3 × 2 = 7x − 6. Set 7x − 6 = 15. Adding 6 gives 7x = 21. Dividing by 7 gives x = 3. Substitution checks it: 7 × 3 − 6 = 15.

Trap. Adding the diagonal products gives 7x + 6 and changes the equation. Subtract 3 × 2.

Memory aid. Main diagonal product minus the other diagonal product.

Question 26

Evaluate 205463891.

  1. −42
    It is the first cofactor contribution alone.
    The contribution from the 2 is −42. The contribution from the 5 is also needed and equals −60.
  2. 102
    It is the result with the sign reversed.
    Reversing the diagonal subtraction in both minors reverses the sign of the determinant. In each minor, use the top-left times bottom-right product first.
  3. 18
    It results from giving the third term a minus sign.
    First-row signs are +, −, +. The third minor is already negative, so its contribution is +5 × (−12) = −60.
  4. −102 ✓
    The nonzero contributions total −42 − 60.

Answer D. Expand across the top row using cofactor signs +, −, +. The middle entry contributes zero. The first term is 2 × 6391 = 2 × (6 − 27) = −42. The third term is 5 × 4689 = 5 × (36 − 48) = −60. The sum is −102.

Trap. The third cofactor has a plus sign. Its minor is negative, so the contribution stays negative.

Memory aid. Expand along a row with zeroes and keep the alternating signs. Across the top row, use plus, minus, plus.
